# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We aim to keep ExecViz constructive, beginner-friendly, and focused on learning. Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable feedback, and do not harass or exclude people.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, and knowingly disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, close, or remove contributions that violate this standard. Report concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub.
